<h1>Sterling Office & Home Report (Power BI)</h1>

<p>
This repository presents an interactive <b>Power BI report</b> analyzing sales, profit, and quantity metrics across 
different regions, categories, and customer segments. The report enables quick insights into performance trends, 
profitability distribution, and sub-category contributions. 
</p>

<p>
Key highlights include:
<ul>
  <li>Regional and segment-wise profit analysis with visual breakdowns.</li>
  <li>Sub-category profitability comparisons identifying top and underperforming areas.</li>
  <li>Trend analysis of profit across years and quarters to capture growth patterns.</li>
  <li>Overall summary showcasing sales (<b>2.30M</b>), profit (<b>286.40K</b>), and quantity (<b>38K</b>).</li>
</ul>
</p>

<p>
This project demonstrates the use of <b>Power BI for business intelligence</b>, emphasizing data-driven decision making 
for retail and office supply sectors. It can be extended to build scalable dashboards for monitoring performance and 
strategic planning. 
</p>
