<h1>📊 Sterling Office & Home Report (Power BI)</h1>

<p>
This repository showcases an interactive <b>Power BI dashboard</b> for analyzing <b>sales, profit, and quantity</b> 
metrics across multiple regions, categories, and customer segments. The report provides actionable insights into 
business performance, profitability distribution, and product-level contributions. 
</p>

<p>
✨ Key Highlights:
<ul>
  <li>🌍 <b>Regional & Segment Analysis</b> – Understand profitability across Central, East, South, and West.</li>
  <li>📈 <b>Trend Insights</b> – Profit tracked by year and quarter for growth evaluation.</li>
  <li>📦 <b>Sub-Category Insights</b> – Identify top-performing (Copiers, Phones) and underperforming products (Tables, Bookcases).</li>
  <li>📊 <b>Business Summary</b> – Sales (<b>2.30M</b>), Profit (<b>286.40K</b>), Quantity (<b>38K</b>).</li>
</ul>
</p>

<p>
This project demonstrates the value of <b>data-driven decision making</b> using Power BI, 
suitable for retail, office supply, and home product performance tracking. 
</p>
